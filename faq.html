FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you rent with us.

How long is the lease term?

Our standard lease is 12 months. Shorter or longer terms can be discussed depending on the property.

How much is the security deposit?

The security deposit is equal to one month's rent and is refundable at the end of your lease, subject to the property condition and local law.

Can I tour a home before applying?

Yes. Book a private tour from the contact page or message us on WhatsApp and we will arrange a time that suits you.

Are the homes furnished?

Most homes are available furnished or semi-furnished. Tell us your preference and we will show matching options.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are considered case by case. Please mention your pet when you enquire so we can confirm for the specific home.

What documents do I need to apply?

A valid photo ID, proof of income or employment, and references. We will share the full checklist when you apply.

Who handles maintenance and repairs?

Our property team handles maintenance requests, so you have a single point of contact for anything the home needs.

How quickly can I move in?

Once your application is approved and the lease is signed, move-in can usually be arranged within a few days.
